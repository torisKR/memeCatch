Refund policy

Effective October 14, 2026 · Product support: toris@toris.kr

1. Merchant of Record

When checkout is enabled, purchases are made from Paddle, which acts as Merchant of Record. Paddle handles payment collection, receipts, applicable taxes, and eligible refunds. Requests may be made through the support link in the Paddle receipt or at paddle.net.

2. General rule

Except where required by applicable law or stated below, completed transactions are non-refundable. This does not limit mandatory consumer rights.

3. Statutory withdrawal rights

Some consumers, including eligible consumers in the EU, EEA, and UK, may have a 14-day statutory withdrawal right. That right may cease once digital service use begins after the buyer gives the consent required by law. Paddle's current refund policy and country-specific buyer terms determine transaction eligibility.

4. Delivery failures

If a technical problem prevents or unreasonably delays delivery of the purchased software access, contact toris@toris.kr. We will first attempt to restore the correct access period. If delivery cannot be completed, the transaction may be refunded through Paddle.

5. How to request help

  • For payment, receipt, tax, or refund requests: use the support link in your Paddle receipt or visit paddle.net.
  • For missing application access or a product defect: email toris@toris.kr with the purchase email, approximate purchase time, and a description of the issue. Do not send payment card details.

6. Processing

Approved refunds are returned by Paddle to the original payment method where possible. Timing depends on Paddle, the payment method, and the buyer's financial institution.
