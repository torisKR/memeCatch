Product tour

A focused research operations workspace

MemeCatch Plus turns recurring monitoring and review work into a visible, account-based workflow.

STEP 01

Create a watchlist

Save the records you need to revisit. Plus removes the free account limit and keeps the watchlist attached to your account.

STEP 02

Review changes

The context watch groups editorial, source, lifecycle, and localization changes so you can identify what requires attention.

STEP 03

Complete follow-up

Use the action inbox and history to open the relevant record, finish the review, and retain a visible activity trail.

Included application modules

Features delivered inside the web app

Personal trend radar

Prioritizes reviewed activity for saved topics and selected interests.

Context change monitor

Surfaces changes to source evidence, usage context, lifecycle state, and reviewed translations.

Weekly delta brief

Summarizes recorded changes across the previous seven-day window.

Action inbox

Collects follow-up items and records completed actions in one place.

Localization queue

Tracks requested, in-review, ready, stale, and source-incomplete language states.

Saved workflow history

Keeps account-level saved records and outcome history available throughout the license period.
